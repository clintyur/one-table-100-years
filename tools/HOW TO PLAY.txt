ONE TABLE, 100 YEARS - a Nom Wah Tea Parlor story
=================================================

WHAT IT IS
A short (about 10 minute) click-through story game for our class presentation.
You sit at one table inside Nom Wah Tea Parlor (13 Doyers Street, Chinatown, NYC).
Every time you do something new - meet the host, look around, order dim sum -
time jumps forward:
1920 > 1930s > 1940s > 1950s-60s > 1970s-80s > 1990s-2000s > 2010 > 2020 > Today.
Click the people around you and they tell you Nom Wah's history.


HOW TO OPEN IT
You do NOT need Claude, any app, an account, or even internet. Just a web browser.

1. Unzip the file you were sent.
   - Mac: double-click the .zip
   - Windows: right-click the .zip > "Extract All" > "Extract"
2. Open the folder and double-click:  One-Table-100-Years.html
3. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). That's it.

Use a laptop or desktop computer (not a phone) for presenting.


HOW TO PLAY
- Click "Take a seat" to start. Turn the sound up: every era has its own music.
- Click the text box (or press the SPACE bar) to read the next line.
- Red pulsing circles = people or things you need to click to keep going.
  Small gold sparkles = optional extras (cars outside the window, radios, etc.).
- When an era is finished, a red button appears in the bottom-right corner
  (for example "Flag down the dim sum lady"). Click it to jump forward in time.
- Along the way you'll give your name, order dim sum, pick a tea, and
  "tap the table" twice to say thank you.
- The ending sums up what kept Nom Wah going for more than 100 years.


PRESENTING TIPS
- FULL SCREEN: click the square-corners button in the top-right corner
  (or press F11 on Windows, Control+Command+F on a Mac).
- START FROM A CERTAIN ERA (to split the presentation): on the title screen,
  click a year next to "Presenting? Jump to:".
- RESTART: click the circular-arrow button in the top-right corner, then OK.
- SOUND ON/OFF: click the speaker button in the top-right corner.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- You see code or a blank page: make sure you unzipped it first, then open
  One-Table-100-Years.html in a web browser (right-click > Open With > Chrome).
- No sound: click the speaker button, and check the computer's volume.
  (Browsers only allow sound after your first click, so it starts with
  "Take a seat".)
- Everything is inside that one .html file. Nothing else needs to be installed.
- School computer won't open files? Backup online version:
  https://clintyur.github.io/one-table-100-years/
