## Deliberately left out

- **Tang v. Tang (2022–)**: this is an active family legal dispute between living people, so the game leaves it out.
- **9/11**: Wilson's personal story is real and serious, so the game leaves it out to keep the tone respectful and the game short.

## Fictional characters

The waiter (1920), Mr. Lee (1940s), the dim sum ladies, the regulars, the film crew, the customers, the server and the tour guide are fictional. The Choys, Wally Tang and Wilson Tang are real; their lines are dramatized from published accounts.
